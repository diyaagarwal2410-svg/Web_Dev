DAL BATI CHURMA

Dal Bati Churma is a classic Rajasthani feast that features spiced lentils (dal), baked wheat dumplings (bati), and a sweet, crumbly dessert (churma). Traditionally, you crumble the bati and pour hot, spiced dal over it, eating it alongside the sweet churma

INGREDIENTS

For the Bati (Dumplings):

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (coarse variety is best)
  • ¼ cup semolina (sooji/rava)
  • ½ cup melted ghee (clarified butter)
  • 1 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)
  • ½ tsp baking powderSalt to tasteWarm water (as needed)

For the Churma (Sweet Crumb):

  • 3-4 pre-baked batis
  • ½ cup powdered sugar (or to taste)
  • ¼ cup ghee¼ cup mixed nuts (almonds, cashews, pistachios)
  • 1 tsp cardamom powder

For the Dal (Lentil Soup):

  • ½ cup mixed lentils (split moong, chana, and tuvar dal)
  • 1 finely chopped onion
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil or gheeSalt and fresh cilantro for garnish

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Making the Bati

    1. Dough Prep:

      2. In a large mixing bowl, combine whole wheat flour, semolina, salt, baking powder, and carom seeds. Add the ½ cup of ghee and mix well using your fingers until the texture resembles breadcrumbs.

    2. Knead:

      3. Gradually add warm water to knead a tight, stiff dough. Cover with a damp cloth and let it rest for 20 minutes.

    3. Shape & Bake:

      4. Divide the dough into equal, golf-ball-sized portions. Lightly flatten them and make a small cross or thumbprint in the center for even cooking. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 30–35 minutes, turning halfway, until golden brown.

    4. Ghee Soak:

      5. While still hot, dip the batis into a bowl of melted ghee. Set aside.

  2. Preparing the Churma

    1. Crush & Roast:

      2. Take 3-4 baked batis and break them into chunks. Pulse them in a food processor or blender to make a coarse powder.

    2. Toast:

      3. Heat ¼ cup ghee in a pan on low heat. Add the powdered bati and roast for 5–7 minutes until aromatic and slightly darker in color.

    3. Sweeten:

      4. Turn off the heat. Let the mixture cool slightly, then stir in the powdered sugar, cardamom powder, and chopped nuts.

  3. Cooking the Dal

    1. Boil:

      2. Rinse the lentils and pressure cook them with 2.5 cups of water, turmeric, and salt until soft and mushy (about 3 whistles).

    2. Tempering (Tadka):

      3. In a separate pot, heat oil/ghee and add cumin seeds. Once they sizzle, add the ginger-garlic paste and onions, cooking until the onions are golden brown.

    3. Spice & Simmer:

      4. Add tomatoes, red chili powder, and garam masala. Cook until the tomatoes are soft and ghee separates. Pour in the cooked lentils and simmer for 10 minutes. Garnish with freshly chopped cilantro.

  4. To Serve

    5. Place the hot, ghee-soaked batis in a bowl, crush them using your hands, and ladle the piping hot dal directly over them. Serve the sweet, crunchy churma on the side, and enjoy with a slice of raw onion and green chili for an authentic Rajasthani experience.
